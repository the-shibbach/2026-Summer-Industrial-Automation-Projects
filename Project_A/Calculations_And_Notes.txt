Calculations:
Need to characterise thermistor approximately using beta-equation.

1/T = 1/T0 + 1/beta ln(R/R0).
At room temperature, 298 K, resistance is 8.5 kOhm.
At body temperature of finger, 308 K, resistance is 6.5 kOhm.
Solving for beta gives a value of approximately 2500 K^-1.


3.3 V - R_fixed - thermistor - GND.
3.3 = I(R_fixed + R_thermistor).
I = 3.3 / (R_fixed + R_thermistor).
so VOUT = I*R_thermistor = (3.3 * R_thermistor) / (R_fixed + R_thermistor).
rearranging gives R_thermistor = (VOUT * R_fixed) / (3.3 - VOUT).


Notes:
Sampling time was chosen to be 1 second to reduce the noise, while also sufficiently capturing the changes caused my holding the thermistor in my hand.
